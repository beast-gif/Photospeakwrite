Drop your form image here (jpg, png, bmp, tif).

`python main.py` will pick up the most recent image in this folder
automatically — no need to type the path.

Tips for best OCR:
- 200 DPI scan or sharper photo
- Even lighting, no glare
- Form roughly square to the camera (deskew handles small tilts)
- Black ink on white paper, printed labels (handwritten labels are harder)
